HERITAGE CYBER SPRINT — QUICK START

ONLINE — MAC OR WINDOWS
Open https://joshuapszh.github.io/heritage-digital-arcade/
Optionally enter a nickname or initials and select Start Sprint.
Press LEFT or RIGHT to play anonymously, start the music and request fullscreen.

OFFLINE — WINDOWS BACKUP
Double-click START-PRESENTATION.cmd.

OFFLINE — MAC BACKUP
Open index.html in Safari or Chrome.

PLAYER CONTROLS
LEFT / RIGHT = enter and choose an answer
DOWN = lock an answer / next student after the achievement
S = teacher settings
M = music on/off
R = reset

The three-lane runner explains Digital Literacy, includes a practice gate, and draws five challenges from a non-repeating pool of 60.
Use the visible settings button to select Junior, Senior or Mixed difficulty and control music, effects, animation, fullscreen, restart and reshuffle.

DEVICE LEADERBOARD
Completed runs are ranked by score and then by sprint time, with a separate top 10 for Junior, Mixed and Senior difficulty. Entries are stored only in that browser. Students should use a nickname or initials, not a full name. Teachers can clear results in Settings.
