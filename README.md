# Heritage Cyber Sprint

## [Launch Heritage Cyber Sprint](https://joshuapszh.github.io/heritage-digital-arcade/)

An original three-lane runner introducing Grade 3–6 students to Digital Literacy at Heritage Academy in about one minute.

### How to play

1. Open the launch link on a Mac or Windows computer.
2. Optionally enter a nickname or initials, then select **Start Sprint**. You can also press **←** or **→** to play anonymously, start the music and request fullscreen.
3. Read each Digital Literacy briefing screen and select **Next**. The **→** or **↓** key also advances.
4. Select **Start Practice** after the final explanation and complete the practice gate.
5. Use **←** and **→** to move across three lanes. Press **↓** to lock in early, or wait for the timed answer gate to arrive.
6. Press **↓** after the achievement screen for the next student.
7. Teachers can click the settings button or press **S** to select Junior, Senior or Mixed difficulty and control audio, animation, reshuffling and fullscreen.

### Device leaderboard

Completed runs are ranked by score and then by sprint time, with a separate top 10 for Junior, Mixed and Senior difficulty. Nickname, score and time entries are stored only in that browser and are never sent online. Students should use a nickname or initials rather than a full name. Teachers can clear all saved results from the settings panel.

Every sprint draws one challenge from each of five curriculum areas: privacy, online safety, digital footprints, information literacy and responsible creativity. The non-repeating bank contains 60 questions with three answer lanes and a teaching explanation after every gate. Music continues between students, accelerates during countdown pressure and changes to a victory arrangement at the finish.

### Offline backup

- Windows: download the repository and run `START-PRESENTATION.cmd`.
- Mac: download the repository and open `index.html` in Safari or Chrome.
